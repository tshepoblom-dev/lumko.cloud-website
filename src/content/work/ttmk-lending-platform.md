## Accuracy is the product

For a lender, the system is the record of who owes what. We built the platform around the loan file, so every disbursement, repayment and document is tied to the loan it belongs to and the history can’t drift between spreadsheets.

## Reconciliation in software

Debit-order collections arrive as files from the collection provider. The platform imports them and matches each line to its loan. Manual reconciliation, a common source of errors and delays, becomes a reviewable step in the system.

## Shaped by the people using it

Back-office software only works when it fits the team’s rhythm. We keep refining the platform with TTMK’s staff: how they capture transactions, how loan terms are recorded, and what management needs to see on the dashboard.
