## From device to decision

Tracking is a chain, and every link matters: the device in the vehicle, the mobile network it reports over, the server that receives and interprets its messages, and the app the operator uses. Building on a proven tracking server let us put our effort where it matters for operators, the experience and the actions they can take.

## Acting, not just watching

Seeing a vehicle on a map is only part of the value. Being able to respond matters more: receiving an alert when a vehicle leaves a geofence, and sending a command to the device from the same app.
